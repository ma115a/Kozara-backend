Stay Guidelines

The Code of Serenity

To preserve the pristine, quiet natural spa atmosphere for all guests, we kindly request observance of our resort values.

Observe Quiet Hours

To honor our absolute zero-noise night sky, we ask all guests to preserve quiet hours between 22:00 and 08:00 hrs. Loud speaker devices are not permitted outdoors during these hours.

Safe Pool Practices

No glass bottles or glassware are allowed directly around the pool borders or stone deck paths. Families must supervise children around pools and open balconies at all times.

Seasonal Readiness

Kozara mountain receives dramatic snowfalls during winter. By local regulations, winter tyres are required starting in November. Basic 4x4 options are suggested for deep snow stays.
