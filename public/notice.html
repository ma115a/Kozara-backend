OPERATING RULES

Terms & Conditions

Confirmations & Booking

A reservation is confirmed when the guest receives written confirmation via email or messaging apps.

Checked Coordinates

Check-In Date: Starting from 14:00 hrs

Check-Out Date: Strict maximum limit 10:00 hrs

Early arrival or late clearance options depend strictly on pre-arrival check-ins.

Settlement & Peak Seasons Cancellations

Payments are settled in full at the time of booking. Local tourist taxes are charged separately at checkout.

Standard Season (Apr - Jun, Mid-Sept - Oct)

  • 25% Refund: Above 14 days before arrival
  • 50% Refund: 7 - 13 days before arrival
  • No refund available under 7 days

Peak Season (July, August, Holidays)

Strict cancellation protection rule: No refunds or cancellations are accepted under peak summer dates or designated holiday weeks.

Pool Safety Disclaimer & Damages

PEVA d.o.o. maintains a pristine luxury standard. Non-compliance with the established house rules may lead to the immediate cancellation of the accommodation. A damage protection deposit of €250 (EUR) is required at check-in.

● Strict Pool Rules (All Units):

  • ✔ Use of pools is entirely at the guest's own risk.
  • ✔ No lifeguard is on duty at the resort garden.
  • ✔ Children must be supervised at all times.
  • ✔ Absolute zero glass permitted near pool areas.
  • ✔ Strict zero diving limit (pool depth 1.40m).

Smoking Policy

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the wooden structures and double-glazed patios. Outdoor terrace smoking is permitted with care.

Pet Accommodations

Domestic pets are accepted upon request prior to arrival. A separate charge of €15.00 per pet per stay is applied.

Occupancy Limits

Maximum of 6 persons per chalet. Layout features 1 double bedroom, 2 twin single rooms, gallery loft options or custom sofa setups.

DATA PROCESSING

Privacy Policy

PEVA d.o.o. collects and processes personal data strictly in accordance with regional registration compliance and legal security parameters.

1. Collected Coordinates & Purpose

We securely register: Full Name, Address, Phone Coordinates, Email registries, and legal ID/passport credentials. This is collected exclusively to fulfill reservation contracts, complete legal guest entry registries, and handle pre-arrival coordinates.

2. Secure Disclosures & Sharing

Your personal details are stored inside secure encryptions and are never sold or shared with commercial agents. Third-party transmissions are restricted only to the Tourist Organisation of Gradiška (legal BA tourism requirement) and processing checkout payment portals.

Under data regulations, guests can request complete deletion of their commercial data profiles post-checkout, provided all legal booking retention tax duties are fulfilled.
